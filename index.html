Who JBO Is For

JBO is designed for middle school baseball players who will attend Corvallis High School (CHS) or Crescent Valley High School (CV). Our program helps players bridge the gap between youth baseball and high school-level expectations.

  • ⚾
    High School Preparation Practice structure and instruction aligned with what players will see at CHS and CV.
  • 📈
    Player Development Focus Emphasis on fundamentals, baseball IQ, and consistent reps.
  • 🤝
    Community & Continuity Build connections with players you'll likely compete alongside in high school.

Williams Field Maintenance

We’re building a small crew to bring Williams Field up to standard for the season. Ideal for anyone who likes hands-on projects.

  • 🌱
    Lawn Care & Groundskeeping Mowing, edging, weeding, and keeping the facility safe and welcoming.
  • 🎨
    Painting Projects Dugouts, railings, and touch-ups to keep the field looking sharp.
  • 🧱
    Fence Repair & Upkeep Minor repairs and cleanup to improve the player and fan experience.
